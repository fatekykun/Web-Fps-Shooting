# Shipment yard update

The previous Shipment map used a 180° mirror of its north half, solid boxes for each container, and an opaque perimeter. This update keeps BREAKPOINT's 36 × 36 m yard and the team spawn rows. It uses the attached blueprint for freight colors, mixed stacking, open cargo, industrial equipment, and the port setting. The layout uses BREAKPOINT's own offset stacks, crossings, and cover placements.

## Container placement

Coordinates are in meters from the yard center. East is +X and south is +Z. The long axis of a container at 0° is parallel to Z, and the standard module is 6 m long, 2.4 m wide, and 2.6 m high. Yaw is around the vertical axis.

The center route bends between the two offset stacks. The west tunnel adds a covered passage between the outer and inner west routes. The east lane passes the forklift and reaches a single-entry storage bay. The north and south cargo screens and low concrete barriers break up views into the spawn yards. All spawn candidates remain available to the existing spawn director.

Container shells and forklift supports share the client and server collision layout. The accessible containers use metal floors and sloped thresholds so normal movement carries players inside. Fence mesh, exterior stacks, and cranes are rendered with instanced geometry. A continuous 5 m collision perimeter keeps the yard contained.
